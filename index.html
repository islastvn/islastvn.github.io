About

Welcome, I hope you enjoy exploring my work! I am in my final year of mechanical engineering at UVic with a specialization in thermo-fluids and aerodynamics. I've spent 12 months on co-op across mechanical design, mechatronics, and instrumentation, and what I enjoy most is taking something from an idea to a working, tested part.

I'm drawn to fields where precision and reliability really matter, especially aerospace and medical device development, where getting it right has a real impact on people's lives. Outside of school, I enjoy hiking, skiing, running, photography, and playing piano.
